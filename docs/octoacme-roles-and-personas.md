# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Executive Sponsors / Business Owners

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors or Business Owners are accountable for the business outcomes of an initiative and provide the organizational authority needed to achieve them.

### Responsibilities
- Set or confirm strategic objectives and expected business outcomes
- Secure or approve funding, capacity, and major scope changes
- Make or escalate decisions that exceed the project team's authority
- Review progress and resolve organizational impediments

### Accountability and Collaboration
- Aligns success measures and desired outcomes with the Product Manager
- Relies on the Project Manager for delivery status, risks, and decision requests
- Provides timely decisions to the team without taking over the Project Manager's day-to-day coordination

### Goals
- Ensure the initiative remains aligned with organizational priorities
- Remove high-level barriers and make decisions at the right level

### Typical Communication
- Project kickoff and milestone reviews
- Executive status summaries and escalations
- Funding, capacity, and scope decision records

---

## Business Analysts / Requirements Leads

### Role Summary
Business Analysts or Requirements Leads clarify stakeholder needs and translate them into traceable, testable requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business needs, workflows, constraints, and assumptions
- Maintain traceability from goals and requirements to acceptance criteria
- Identify requirement gaps, dependencies, and conflicting stakeholder needs
- Clarify expected behavior and edge cases throughout delivery

### Accountability and Collaboration
- Works with the Product Manager to refine requirements; the Product Manager remains accountable for product priorities and backlog decisions
- Clarifies requirements with Developers and QA/Test Leads so implementation and verification share the same understanding
- Coordinates stakeholder input and requirement-related dependencies with the Project Manager

### Goals
- Make requirements understandable, complete, and verifiable
- Reduce avoidable rework caused by ambiguity or missed needs

### Typical Communication
- Requirements and process-flow documentation
- Backlog refinement and acceptance-criteria discussions
- Stakeholder interviews and clarification sessions

---

## UX Researchers / Designers

### Role Summary
UX Researchers and Designers investigate user needs and shape accessible, usable experiences that address those needs.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research and synthesize findings
- Create and validate interaction flows, prototypes, and design guidance
- Identify usability and accessibility considerations
- Share design decisions and evidence with the delivery team

### Accountability and Collaboration
- Partners with the Product Manager to connect user evidence to product outcomes and priorities
- Works with Developers to assess feasibility and clarify design intent during implementation
- Coordinates usability and accessibility validation with QA/Test Leads
- Shares research and design dependencies with the Project Manager for planning

### Goals
- Ensure solutions are grounded in user needs
- Make experiences usable and accessible

### Typical Communication
- Research plans, findings, and user feedback
- Design reviews, prototypes, and design specifications
- Usability and accessibility review sessions

---

## QA / Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA or Test Leads coordinate quality practices and provide evidence about whether delivered work meets its requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Define test scope, strategy, and coverage for project risks
- Coordinate functional, integration, regression, and acceptance testing as appropriate
- Track defects, verify fixes, and communicate unresolved quality risks
- Report test results and release-readiness evidence

### Accountability and Collaboration
- Works with Developers to prevent defects, investigate failures, and verify fixes
- Confirms testability and expected outcomes with the Product Manager and Business Analyst
- Coordinates test timing and communicates quality-related schedule risks with the Project Manager
- Shares usability, accessibility, and security verification needs with UX and Security/Privacy Leads

### Goals
- Detect important defects and risks early
- Provide transparent evidence for acceptance and release decisions

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, defect reports, and test results
- Quality-risk and release-readiness updates
- Acceptance-criteria and defect-triage discussions

---

## Security / Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads identify applicable security, privacy, and compliance needs and coordinate their review throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Identify relevant security, privacy, and compliance requirements and risks
- Advise on mitigations, safeguards, and required reviews
- Coordinate security and privacy assessments and track resulting actions
- Escalate unresolved or unacceptable risks to the designated decision-maker

### Accountability and Collaboration
- Works with the Product Manager and Business Analyst to capture requirements early and assess product trade-offs
- Advises Developers on secure implementation and privacy safeguards
- Coordinates verification needs with QA/Test Leads
- Records risks and review dependencies with the Project Manager for tracking and escalation

### Goals
- Reduce the likelihood and impact of security and privacy issues
- Make compliance obligations and residual risks visible before release

### Typical Communication
- Security and privacy requirements or assessment findings
- Risk and mitigation tracking
- Review, remediation, and escalation discussions

---

## Release / Operations Owners

### Role Summary
Release or Operations Owners coordinate deployment and operational readiness so changes can be supported and monitored after release.

### Responsibilities
- Define deployment, monitoring, rollback, and support-readiness needs
- Coordinate operational checklists and release dependencies
- Confirm that required runbooks, alerts, and support handoffs are prepared
- Share production feedback and operational risks with the project team

### Accountability and Collaboration
- Works with Developers and QA/Test Leads to confirm deployment steps and release criteria
- Coordinates the release schedule, dependencies, and stakeholder communications with the Project Manager
- Shares operational constraints and production feedback with the Product Manager to inform priorities
- Works with Security/Privacy Leads on operational controls and incident considerations

### Goals
- Make releases predictable and supportable
- Detect and respond to operational issues effectively

### Typical Communication
- Release plans, readiness checklists, and runbooks
- Deployment and incident communications
- Monitoring results and post-release feedback

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- In smaller projects, one person may cover multiple personas; keep responsibilities and decision ownership explicit.
